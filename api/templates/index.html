Export courses from Canvas

Connect with an admin token to see every course in your account, then export course content, people, grades and discussions in one go.

In Canvas, go to Account, Settings, then New access token.
Your token is kept in an encrypted cookie that page scripts can't read. It expires after 8 hours and is removed when you disconnect or lock the app.
